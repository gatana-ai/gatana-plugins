# Gatana

Use the skills of your organization in Claude. A skill tells Claude how a type of work is done in your organization: how to deploy a service, how to review a pull request, how to investigate an error.

## Before you start

You need a Gatana account. When you connect the plugin, you choose your organization and sign in. No account yet? Sign up at https://hello.gatana.ai/signup.

## Use it

1. Open the plugin and connect the Gatana connector. Sign in with your Gatana account.
2. Start a task as usual. Claude checks the skills of your organization first, and follows the skill that applies.
3. To keep a way of working for your team, ask Claude to save it as a Gatana skill.

You see only the skills that you have access to in Gatana.

## Data

The plugin connects Claude to the Gatana gateway of your organization. Claude sends skill searches, skill names and new skill texts to it, and receives the skills that you can read. The gateway also gives Claude the tools of the MCP servers that your organization connected to Gatana. The plugin itself stores nothing.

More information: https://docs.gatana.ai/skills/
